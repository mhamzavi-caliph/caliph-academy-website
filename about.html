Who We Are

Where Islamic values meet modern business

Caliph Academy is dedicated to nurturing a new generation of professionals who lead with both competence and character. We believe that authentic Islamic scholarship and rigorous business education belong together — and our Double Diploma in Business Administration (Muamalat) is built on exactly that conviction.

Caliph Academy (Caliph Life Sdn Bhd) is the appointed Centre of Education (COE) delivering this programme in collaboration with UTM SPACE, the School of Professional and Continuing Education of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). Our students enrol as UTM SPACE students and study at UTM Kuala Lumpur (Semarak).

By pairing UTM SPACE and MQA recognition with the London Examinations Board, we give our students qualifications that are trusted at home and respected abroad.
