Admissions

Applications for the December 2026 intake are open. Here's everything you need to join Caliph Academy.

Entry Requirements

Who can apply

General guidelines for the Double Diploma in Business Administration (Muamalat). Our admissions team will confirm eligibility for your specific qualifications.

📘

Academic Pathway (SPM)

A pass in SPM with at least 3 credits in any subjects, or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Malaysian government.

📗

Certificate Pathway

A relevant Certificate (Level 3, MQF) with a minimum CGPA as required, providing credit-transfer into the diploma.

🌐

Language

A pass in Bahasa Melayu and a credit or pass in English at SPM level supports success in the programme.

💼

Working Adults (Part-Time)

Professionals in banking, insurance or finance may qualify through APEL (accreditation of prior experiential learning).

Requirements are indicative and subject to Caliph Academy and MQA regulations. Contact us to confirm your eligibility.

How to Apply

Four simple steps

Submit your enquiry

Complete the form below or contact our admissions team to register your interest and choose full-time or part-time study.

Prepare your documents

Gather your SPM/certificate results, identity document and a passport photo ready for your application.

Complete your application

Our team guides you through the application form and confirms your eligibility and intake date.

Enrol & begin

Accept your offer, complete registration and start your journey toward a double diploma.
