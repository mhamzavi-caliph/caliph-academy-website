What is LOYLA?

Lead beyond borders at the University of Oxford

LOYLA — the London-Oxford Youth Leadership Award — is the exclusive international leadership programme woven into the Double Diploma, hosted at the prestigious University of Oxford. It empowers you with the skills, confidence and global perspective to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Through engaging seminars, experiential activities and cultural exploration, you develop leadership, communication, critical thinking and teamwork — while building friendships and memories that last a lifetime.

Programme Structure

10 days. 8 nights. One extraordinary journey.

Your LOYLA expedition to Oxford & London — 6–15 December 2026. Itinerary subject to change.

Oxford & London Expedition

10 Days / 8 Nights · December 2026
  • Depart Kuala Lumpur, arrive in London & transfer to Oxford
  • Leadership Seminar at the University of Oxford · Oxford Westgate Innovation Hunt · Group Assessment · Closing Ceremony
  • Transfer to London & check in to your accommodation
  • Farewell picnic at Malaysia Hall, London · explore London at your own pace · optional Harry Potter Studio excursion
  • Transfer to Heathrow & depart for Kuala Lumpur
  • Arrive in Kuala Lumpur

LOYLA Flight Scholarship

Available to Double Diploma programme students only.

Flight Ticket · KL – London – KL RM0 Free of charge

The London-Oxford Youth Leadership Award (LOYLA) is a 10-day leadership mobility programme at the University of Oxford and London, designed to cultivate leadership, critical thinking and global exposure.

The return flight (Kuala Lumpur–London–Kuala Lumpur) is covered by the LOYLA Flight Scholarship for Double Diploma students. An optional Harry Potter Studio excursion is available on request.
