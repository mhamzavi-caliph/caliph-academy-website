In Collaboration with UTMSpace Services Sdn Bhd

You study as a UTMSpace Services Sdn Bhd student

This Double Diploma is delivered together with UTMSpace Services Sdn Bhd — the School of Professional and Continuing Education of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM), one of Malaysia's premier public universities. Caliph Academy (Caliph Life Sdn Bhd) is the appointed Marketing Partner and Centre of Education (COE) for UTMSpace Services Sdn Bhd, delivering the programme.

  • Enrol and graduate as a registered UTMSpace Services Sdn Bhd student
  • Study on campus at UTM Kuala Lumpur (Semarak)
  • A UTM-recognised diploma, taught by qualified educators
  • A real university experience — not just a certificate
