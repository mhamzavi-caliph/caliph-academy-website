The Programme
About the Programme

Business administration, grounded in Shariah

The Islamic finance and Muamalat sectors — spanning Islamic insurance and Shariah-compliant commercial dealings — are among the fastest-growing areas of global finance. This Double Diploma prepares you to work in them with confidence, combining core business administration with authentic Islamic jurisprudence, and awards you two recognised qualifications from one integrated curriculum.

Recognised Three Ways

MQA (Malaysia)

Accredited under the Malaysian Qualifications Framework. MQA/PA18855.

London Exam. Board (UK)

A parallel UK-benchmarked award for international standing.

UTM SPACE

Delivered with UTM SPACE — you study as a UTM student.

In collaboration with UTM SPACE

Caliph Academy (Caliph Life Sdn Bhd) is the appointed Centre of Education (COE) delivering this programme with UTM SPACE, the School of Professional and Continuing Education of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). Students enrol and graduate as registered UTM SPACE students and study on campus at UTM Kuala Lumpur (Semarak).
