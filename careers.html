Continue Studying

Articulate into a degree — here or abroad

Because your diploma is recognised by MQA and the London Examinations Board, you can transfer credits into bachelor's programmes both in Malaysia and internationally.

  • Malaysian public & private universities (Islamic finance, business, accounting)
  • UK & international universities via the London Examinations Board pathway
  • Advanced standing — often entering Year 2 or Year 3
  • Onward to Master's degrees and professional certifications (e.g. IFP, CIFP)
