Double Diploma in Business Administration
(Muamalat)

One programme awarding two recognised qualifications — MQA-accredited in Malaysia and awarded with the London Examinations Board in the UK.

Programme Overview

Business administration, grounded in Shariah

The Islamic finance and Muamalat sectors — spanning Islamic insurance and Shariah-compliant commercial dealings — are among the fastest-growing areas of global finance. This diploma prepares you to work in them with confidence — combining core business administration with authentic Islamic jurisprudence.

Across the programme you'll master accounting, risk management, marketing and entrepreneurship, while building deep fluency in Fiqh Muamalat, Usul Fiqh and the principles that govern Shariah-compliant business.

  • 27 courses across Shariah, business and professional skills
  • Dedicated industrial training placement
  • Full-time (2 years) or part-time (4 years) study
  • Two awards from one integrated curriculum

The Double Diploma Advantage

How two qualifications work together

You study one integrated curriculum and graduate with credentials recognised both nationally and internationally.

Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA)

Your diploma is accredited under the Malaysian Qualifications Framework (MQF) — the national standard trusted by employers, universities and government agencies. It provides a clear articulation pathway into bachelor's degree programmes across Malaysia.

MQA Approval: MQA/PA18855

London Examinations Board (UK)

A UK-based awarding organisation delivering internationally benchmarked qualifications. Your LEB Advanced Diploma adds global credibility to your profile and opens pathways to further study and professional recognition beyond Malaysia.

Our UK Awarding Partner

Who is the London Examinations Board?

The London Examinations Board (LEB) is a UK-based awarding organisation, headquartered in Hertfordshire, England. It designs and awards internationally recognised qualifications — from higher and professional diplomas through to degree and postgraduate pathways delivered with partner universities worldwide.

LEB's vision is to provide "highly flexible, highly recognised and high-quality programmes at highly competitive prices worldwide" — pairing every qualification with clear progression routes. For Caliph Academy students, that means a Malaysian diploma matched by a globally portable UK credential.

  • UK-based awarding body, headquartered in Hertfordshire, England
  • Internationally benchmarked diplomas with university progression pathways
  • Registered on the UK Register of Learning Providers (UKRLP)
  • Member of the Hertfordshire Chamber of Commerce
  • University pathway partners across the UK and USA
